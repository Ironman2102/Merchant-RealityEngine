# Two-minute stall pitch

Every number is from `reports/`. Say "synthetic" out loud.

**0:00 The problem.** A bakery passes KYC with real papers and then takes bets. The ED alleges betting money moved through UPI, mule
or merchant accounts in the [1xBet](https://www.enforcementdirectorate.gov.in/media/press-release-documents/9c952e26-2ade-452d-9a44-cd180d548566_Press%20Release%20-PAO-1xbet%20-2%203%20%202026%201.pdf) and [Parimatch](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/ed-raids-12-locations-across-4-states-in-parimatch-illegal-betting-money-laundering-probe) cases. Since 1 May 2026 the [Online Gaming Act](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2254606&reg=3&lang=1) bars
payment systems from carrying it, and [RBI already requires](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896) monitoring against the business profile. The rule exists.
The hard part is doing it without hurting honest shops.

**0:20 Our two ideas.** "We don't score the shop. We score every QR." and "A change is a question. Misconduct is a case."

**0:35 Golden Oven.** A busy, real bakery. On average it looks fine. On its own, the second link is round top-ups from the same few
distant people. A case on that link only. The counter keeps selling.

**1:05 Moonlight Bakes.** It started midnight delivery. A threshold system flags it. We ask. Click "Simulate second-person check":
the Context Passport clears it, and it could not have cleared Golden Oven.

**1:30 Anand Sweets.** Three times the sales at Diwali. No case and no question.

**1:40 Honesty.** On 1,000 synthetic merchants: 0 of 919 honest merchants wrongly opened; 33 of 81 fronts opened and 67 of 81
opened or questioned. A perfect mimic beats payment data. A 90-day shadow pilot is how we get real numbers.

**1:55 Ask.** "Give us 90 days of one portfolio in shadow mode. We change nothing and show you what we would have asked."
