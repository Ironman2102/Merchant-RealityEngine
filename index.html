What MRE does

Watch the money, not the paperwork.

Learns normal

What a bakery, kirana store or salon should look like: ticket sizes, hours, payer geography and settlement speed for each category.

Spots misfits

28 statistical detectors in 10 evidence families, scored for every QR code and payment link. Side business can't hide in shop averages.

Opens cases on evidence

A case opens only when two independent evidence families agree. A new menu or a festival rush gets a question, not a freeze.

Smallest safe step

From "ask the merchant" up to pausing one QR code. Bigger steps need a second human approver.

Shows its working

Every flag carries its evidence. Every action is sealed in a tamper-evident, hash-chained audit log.

AI as a second opinion

LightGBM + SHAP, neural nets, a temporal CNN and an autoencoder score every case and explain why. Decisions stay with the evidence.

How MRE decides

Seven layers. One explainable call.

L1
Ingest & lineage

Payments, refunds, settlements, links and KYC in one record. A missing feed stays "unknown", never "clean".

L2
Merchant profile

Category norms for ticket size, timing and payers. The merchant's own claim is checked.

L3
Detection

28 tests across amounts, payers & places, timing, money flow, networks, links & website.

L4
Evidence fusion

Bayesian maths combines families without double-counting and finds rings across merchants.

L5
Analyst review

Honest and suspicious explanations side by side, plus Stage 2 ML scores and their reasons.

L6
Governed response

The smallest action that works. Bigger steps need a second approver.

L7
Audit & learning

Hash-chained log. Confirmed outcomes retrain the tests and models.
